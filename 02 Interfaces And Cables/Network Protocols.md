# 🗣️ Network Protocols

> [!abstract] Why protocols matter
> Communication requires an **agreed-upon system** — like a common language between two people.

## 🌍 The Language Analogy

![[Pasted image 20261112143953.png]]

If one person only speaks English and the other only speaks Japanese, there will be little or no communication. What they need is a shared, agreed-upon system of communicating — a **common language**.

## 🔌 From Language to Hardware

![[Pasted image 20261112144123.png]]

If you try to connect a cable to a switch, but the cable maker and the switch maker never agreed on the **size and shape** of the connector and port, they won't fit.

> [!important] Standards make it work
> Industry standards — followed by all vendors — cover both:
> - **Physical standards** — connectors and cables
> - **Logical standards** — protocols such as **IP** (Internet Protocol)

## 🧱 Two Layers of Standards

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical** | Connector shape, port size, cable types |
| **Logical** | IP, Ethernet rules, communication protocols |

> [!success] Key takeaway
> Protocols and standards are the shared rules that let hardware and software from different vendors interoperate.
