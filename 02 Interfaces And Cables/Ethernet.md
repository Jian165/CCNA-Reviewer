# 🧵 Ethernet

> [!abstract] Definition
> Ethernet is a **collection of network protocols and standards**, not just a single protocol.

## 📦 What Ethernet Covers

- Cabling as defined by Ethernet standards
- The rules and formats devices use to communicate on a LAN
- Physical connectors, such as the [[UTP Cable|RJ-45]]

> [!tip] Think of it as...
> A rulebook for how data is framed, addressed, and sent across a local network.

> - [[Interfaces And Cables]] — RJ-45 connectors and ports
> - [[Network Protocols]] — why agreed-upon standards matter

> [!success] Key takeaway
> Ethernet is a **family** of standards covering both the physical cabling and the logical rules for LAN communication.
