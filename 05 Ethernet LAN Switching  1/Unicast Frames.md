Due to lack of space I’ve just written an abbreviated form of the destination and source MAC addresses here.  
By the way, this kind of frame is called a ‘unicast frame’, a frame destined for a single target, PC2 in this case.  
There are other kinds of frames, like broadcast frames, which we’ll learn as we go along. But for now, remember this term, unicast.  
PC1 sends the frame through it’s network interface card, which is connected to SW1, and SW1 receives the frame.
